Process

Not project by project.
Together as partners.

GLANZH offers agile retainers tailored precisely to individual business needs. Here is how collaboration unfolds.

  1. Kennenlernen & Bedarfsanalyse

    We understand before we create. In our introductory conversation, we clarify what is needed, at what frequency, and across which touchpoints – designing a partnership model that truly fits.

  2. Angebot & Retainer-Modell

    No rigid packages. We collaboratively define a flexible model tailored to your exact demands – predictable, transparent, and with no hidden fees.

  3. Onboarding & Brand Handover

    We immerse ourselves in your brand – design system, guidelines, legacy assets, and workflows. Ensuring total brand consistency from day one.

  4. Laufende Zusammenarbeit

    Reach out whenever needs arise – we are right there. Direct, fast, proactive. No lengthy briefing red-tape, no account-handler middle layers.

  5. Continuous Alignment

    We maintain an ongoing dialogue – discussing active initiatives, emerging demands, and ways to continuously refine our partnership.
